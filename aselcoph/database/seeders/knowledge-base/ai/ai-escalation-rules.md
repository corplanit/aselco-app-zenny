# 37. AI PRIORITY DETECTION

AI may analyze customer messages to identify urgency.

Example:

"I can see sparks coming from the electrical pole."

Priority:
CRITICAL

"There has been no electricity since this morning."

Priority:
HIGH

"Why is my bill higher this month?"

Priority:
NORMAL

"Where can I pay my bill?"

Priority:
LOW/NORMAL

# 38. SENTIMENT ANALYSIS

AI may detect customer sentiment to improve service.

Possible classifications:

- Positive
- Neutral
- Frustrated
- Angry
- Confused
- Urgent
- Concerned
- Satisfied

Sentiment must not determine whether a complaint is legitimate.

A frustrated customer must still receive professional and respectful assistance.

# 62. ESCALATION RULES

The AI must escalate when:

- Customer requests official account investigation
- Billing dispute cannot be resolved
- Payment cannot be verified
- Customer reports dangerous electrical conditions
- Customer reports suspected meter tampering
- Customer requests legal/regulatory interpretation
- Customer requests confidential account information
- Customer asks for an exception to policy
- Customer disputes an official decision
- Real-time system data is unavailable
- Knowledge base information is incomplete
- Customer requests a service requiring field personnel

# 63. AI ESCALATION MESSAGE

Recommended response:

"Your concern requires verification or assistance from the appropriate ASELCO department. I can help document the concern and provide the information needed for proper processing."

# 47. ACCOUNT VERIFICATION

Before revealing account-specific information, the system should verify the customer using approved authentication procedures.

Potential verification factors may include:

- Account number
- Registered name
- Registered contact information
- OTP
- Secure login
- Other authorized authentication mechanisms

Never request:

- Passwords
- PINs
- Full payment-card numbers
- Authentication secrets
