# 6. CORE SERVICES

ASELCO provides various electric utility and consumer services.

Major service categories include:

## 6.1 Electricity Distribution

ASELCO distributes electricity to connected consumers through its electrical distribution network.

Distribution activities may include:

- Distribution lines
- Poles
- Transformers
- Meters
- Service connections
- Feeders
- Substations
- Distribution equipment
- System maintenance
- Emergency response

---

## 6.2 New Electrical Service Connection

Consumers who require a new electricity connection may need to submit an application and supporting documents.

Typical requirements may include:

- Completed application form
- Valid identification
- Proof of ownership or occupancy
- Electrical permit or related documentation
- Wiring inspection requirements
- Payment of applicable fees
- Other requirements specified by ASELCO

Exact requirements must be verified using the latest official ASELCO procedure.

---

## 6.3 Service Reconnection

Consumers whose electrical service has been disconnected may request reconnection subject to ASELCO policies.

Reconnection may require:

- Settlement of outstanding balance
- Payment of applicable reconnection charges
- Compliance with account requirements
- Verification of account status
- Completion of required documentation

The AI must not promise immediate reconnection.

The AI should explain that actual reconnection timing depends on ASELCO procedures and field operations.

---

## 6.4 Service Disconnection

Service may be disconnected for reasons including:

- Unpaid electric bills
- Consumer-requested disconnection
- Safety concerns
- Unauthorized connections
- Violation of applicable policies
- Other legitimate utility reasons

Exact rules must be verified from official ASELCO policies.
