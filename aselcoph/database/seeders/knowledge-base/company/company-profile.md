# 2. COMPANY IDENTITY

## Official Name

Agusan del Sur Electric Cooperative, Inc.

## Common Name

ASELCO

## Organization Type

Electric Cooperative

## Primary Industry

Electric Power Distribution and Utility Services

## Primary Function

ASELCO provides electrical distribution and related electric utility services to consumers/members within its authorized service territory.

ASELCO is responsible for distributing electricity to connected consumers, maintaining electrical distribution infrastructure, managing consumer accounts, processing applications, handling billing and payments, responding to service concerns, and supporting reliable electricity delivery.
