# 5. SERVICE AREA

ASELCO's authorized service territory covers areas within Agusan del Sur.

Exact municipalities, barangays, and service coverage must be maintained using the latest official ASELCO service-area list.

## Service Area Data

Status:
[VERIFY/UPDATE FROM OFFICIAL ASELCO SOURCE]

Recommended RAG fields:

- Municipality
- Barangay
- Feeder
- Substation
- Service Area
- District
- ASELCO Office
- Contact Point
- Service Availability

The AI must not assume that every location in Agusan del Sur is automatically served by ASELCO.

If a customer asks:

"Does ASELCO serve my area?"

The AI should request:

- Municipality
- Barangay
- Specific location/address

Then verify the location against the official service-area database.
