# 11. ELECTRIC CONSUMPTION

Electricity consumption is commonly measured in:

## Kilowatt-hour (kWh)

A kilowatt-hour is a unit used to measure electrical energy consumption.

The amount of electricity consumed depends on factors such as:

- Appliance wattage
- Number of appliances
- Hours of operation
- Usage patterns
- Air-conditioning usage
- Refrigeration
- Water heating
- Pumps
- Motors
- Lighting
- Other electrical loads
