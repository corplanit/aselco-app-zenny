# 10. BILLING

ASELCO billing determines the amount payable for electricity consumed and applicable charges.

A customer's bill may contain:

- Previous balance
- Current consumption
- Meter reading
- Consumption in kWh
- Energy charge
- Distribution-related charges
- Government charges
- Taxes
- Other approved charges
- Adjustments
- Payments
- Amount due
- Due date

Exact bill components and current charges must be retrieved from the latest official billing/rate information.

# 17. DUE DATE

The bill due date is the date by which payment should be made according to the customer's billing statement.

The AI should always use the account's actual billing data when available.

Never invent a due date.

If no account data is available, instruct the customer to check their latest billing statement or contact ASELCO.
