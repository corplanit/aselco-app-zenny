# 12. HIGH ELECTRIC BILL

Customers may report unexpectedly high electricity bills.

The AI should investigate possible causes.

Possible causes include:

1. Increased electricity usage
2. Longer appliance operating time
3. Additional appliances
4. Air-conditioning usage
5. Heating equipment
6. Refrigeration issues
7. Water pumps
8. Electrical leakage
9. Appliance malfunction
10. Meter reading differences
11. Estimated reading
12. Previous unpaid balance
13. Adjustments
14. Rate changes
15. Seasonal consumption changes
16. Changes in household/business activity

The AI should not automatically claim that a high bill is caused by a defective meter.

Recommended response:

- Review the bill
- Compare current and previous kWh consumption
- Check appliance usage
- Verify meter reading
- Check account balance
- Request official billing investigation when necessary

# 13. BILLING DISPUTE

A consumer may request an investigation when they believe their bill is incorrect.

Possible reasons:

- Unexpected increase
- Incorrect account information
- Suspected meter-reading issue
- Payment not reflected
- Duplicate payment concern
- Incorrect balance
- Unrecognized charge
- Other billing discrepancy

Recommended workflow:

1. Verify consumer account.
2. Identify the billing period.
3. Review current consumption.
4. Review previous consumption.
5. Review meter reading.
6. Review payments.
7. Review adjustments.
8. Determine whether the issue can be explained from available data.
9. Escalate to the appropriate ASELCO department if investigation is required.
10. Provide the customer with a reference/ticket number when available.
