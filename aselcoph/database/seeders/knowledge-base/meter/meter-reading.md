# 19. METERING

Electric meters measure electricity consumption at a customer's service connection.

Meter-related concerns may include:

- Meter reading
- Meter malfunction
- Damaged meter
- Broken meter seal
- Suspected tampering
- Meter replacement
- Meter installation
- Meter inspection
- Unusual consumption
- Meter accuracy concerns

# 20. METER READING

A meter reading represents the measured electricity consumption used to calculate billing.

Customers may ask why their meter reading increased.

Possible explanations include:

- Higher consumption
- More operating hours
- Additional appliances
- Seasonal usage
- Previous estimated reading
- Corrected reading
- Meter replacement
- Billing adjustment

The AI should avoid making technical conclusions without official meter data.
