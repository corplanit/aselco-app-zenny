# 54. FAQ — GENERAL

## What is ASELCO?

ASELCO stands for Agusan del Sur Electric Cooperative, Inc., an electric cooperative responsible for electricity distribution and related consumer services within its authorized service area.

## What services does ASELCO provide?

ASELCO provides electricity distribution and consumer-related services such as new connections, account services, billing, payment assistance, outage response, meter services, reconnection, and complaint handling.

## How can I contact ASELCO?

Use the latest official ASELCO contact information maintained in the organization's official channels.

## How can I report an outage?

Provide your location and account information when available, then report the outage through the official ASELCO customer-service or outage-reporting channel.

# 55. FAQ — BILLING

## Why did my electric bill increase?

Possible causes include higher electricity consumption, longer appliance usage, additional electrical loads, seasonal changes, meter-reading differences, adjustments, or other billing factors.

## How can I check my bill?

Use the official ASELCO billing/customer account system or your latest billing statement.

## I think my bill is incorrect. What should I do?

Request a billing investigation through the official ASELCO customer-service channel.

## Why is my payment not reflected?

Payment posting may require processing time. Provide your account number, payment date, amount, payment channel, and transaction reference for verification.

# 56. FAQ — OUTAGE

## There is no electricity in my house. What should I do?

Check whether neighboring properties are also affected. Report the outage to ASELCO through the official channel and provide your exact location.

## How long will the outage last?

Restoration time depends on the cause and extent of the outage. The AI should provide an estimated restoration time only when officially available.

## There are fallen electrical wires. What should I do?

Stay away from the wires and keep other people away. Treat fallen wires as energized and report the incident immediately through the appropriate emergency/ASELCO channels.

# 57. FAQ — NEW CONNECTION

## How do I apply for a new connection?

Contact or visit the appropriate ASELCO office/customer-service channel and submit the required application documents.

## What documents are required?

Requirements may include identification, proof of ownership or occupancy, electrical documents, permits, and other ASELCO requirements. The latest official checklist should be used.

## How much does a new connection cost?

Fees vary according to applicable ASELCO rules and service requirements. The AI must retrieve the current official fee schedule rather than provide an outdated amount.

# 58. FAQ — RECONNECTION

## My electricity was disconnected. How can I reconnect?

Check the account status and outstanding balance, settle applicable charges, and follow the official ASELCO reconnection procedure.

## Will my electricity be restored immediately after payment?

Not necessarily. Reconnection depends on payment posting, verification, applicable procedures, and field operations.

# 59. FAQ — METER

## Can I open my electric meter?

No. Customers should not open, alter, bypass, or manipulate the electric meter.

## My meter appears damaged. What should I do?

Do not touch or attempt to repair it. Report the problem to ASELCO.

## My electricity consumption seems unusually high. What should I do?

Compare current and previous consumption, check appliance usage, and request a meter/billing investigation if necessary.
