## 6.2 New Electrical Service Connection

Consumers who require a new electricity connection may need to submit an application and supporting documents.

Typical requirements may include:

- Completed application form
- Valid identification
- Proof of ownership or occupancy
- Electrical permit or related documentation
- Wiring inspection requirements
- Payment of applicable fees
- Other requirements specified by ASELCO

Exact requirements must be verified using the latest official ASELCO procedure.

---

# 29. NEW CONNECTION

A new connection allows an eligible consumer to receive electrical service at a new location.

General process:

1. Submit application.
2. Provide required documents.
3. Electrical installation is prepared.
4. Required inspection/approval is completed.
5. Applicable fees are paid.
6. Service connection is scheduled.
7. Meter is installed.
8. Electricity service is energized.

Exact process and requirements may vary.

## ASELCO App — Service Connection

New service connection and reconnection requests must be filed as a concern in the app or logged by CSR intake.
Membership setup requires linking the electric account number for validation by staff.
Only validated account links can be used for AST bill payment.
Service documentation does not authorize the AI to approve connections or change membership status.
