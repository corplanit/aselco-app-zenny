# 30. CHANGE OF ACCOUNT NAME

A consumer may request a change of account name because of:

- Property transfer
- New owner
- Change in occupancy
- Legal name change
- Other valid circumstances

Supporting documentation may be required.

The AI should provide the latest official requirements.

# 31. TRANSFER OF SERVICE

If a customer moves to another property, the old service account may require closure or transfer procedures while the new location may require a new connection.

The AI should not assume that an account can simply be transferred without official processing.

# 32. SERVICE REQUESTS

Common service requests include:

- New connection
- Reconnection
- Disconnection
- Meter inspection
- Meter concern
- Billing investigation
- Payment verification
- Account update
- Name change
- Service information
- Outage report
- Damaged equipment report
- Other utility concerns

Each request should receive an appropriate category and priority.
