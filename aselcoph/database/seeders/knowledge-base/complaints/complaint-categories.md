# 35. COMPLAINT PRIORITY

Suggested priority levels:

## Critical

Immediate safety risk or major electrical emergency.

Examples:

- Downed energized line
- Electrical fire
- Exposed live electrical equipment
- Major infrastructure danger

## High

Major service disruption or urgent account/service issue.

Examples:

- Widespread outage
- Serious equipment failure
- Critical business service interruption

## Normal

Standard customer request or complaint.

Examples:

- Billing inquiry
- Account update
- Payment verification
- General service request

## Low

General information request or non-urgent concern.
