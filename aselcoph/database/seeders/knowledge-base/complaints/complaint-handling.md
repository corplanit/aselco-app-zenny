# 33. CUSTOMER COMPLAINTS

A complaint is a formal expression of dissatisfaction regarding an ASELCO service, process, bill, account, employee interaction, outage, or other concern.

Complaint categories may include:

- Billing complaint
- Payment complaint
- Meter complaint
- Outage complaint
- Service complaint
- New connection complaint
- Reconnection complaint
- Disconnection complaint
- Staff/customer-service complaint
- Technical complaint
- Other complaint

# 34. COMPLAINT HANDLING WORKFLOW

Recommended workflow:

1. Receive complaint.
2. Identify customer.
3. Verify account when required.
4. Categorize complaint.
5. Record complaint details.
6. Determine urgency.
7. Assign responsible department.
8. Generate ticket/reference number.
9. Investigate.
10. Provide customer updates.
11. Resolve.
12. Record resolution.
13. Close ticket.
14. Collect feedback when applicable.

# 64. TICKET INFORMATION

A service ticket may contain:

- Ticket ID
- Account number
- Consumer name
- Category
- Subcategory
- Description
- Location
- Priority
- Status
- Date/time created
- Assigned department
- Assigned personnel
- Attachments
- Customer updates
- Resolution
- Date/time resolved
- Closing notes

# 65. TICKET STATUS DEFINITIONS

## Open

The concern has been received but has not yet been resolved.

## In Progress

The assigned team is actively working on the concern.

## Pending

Additional information, verification, payment, documentation, or action is required.

## Escalated

The concern has been forwarded to another department or higher-level personnel.

## Resolved

The concern has been addressed.

## Closed

The ticket has completed the official workflow.

## Cancelled

The request was cancelled or determined to be invalid according to the applicable procedure.

# 66. ATTACHMENTS

Customers may be required to submit attachments for certain requests.

Possible documents include:

- Proof of payment
- Application documents
- Identification
- Property documents
- Electrical permits
- Photos of damaged equipment
- Supporting documents
- Official forms

The system should validate file type and size according to the application's security policy.
