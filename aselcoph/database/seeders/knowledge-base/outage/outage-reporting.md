# 23. POWER OUTAGE

A power outage is an interruption of electrical service.

Possible causes include:

- Equipment failure
- Distribution line damage
- Transformer problems
- Scheduled maintenance
- Emergency maintenance
- Weather conditions
- Fallen trees
- Vehicle accidents
- Animals contacting equipment
- System faults
- Overloaded equipment
- Other grid or distribution events

The AI should distinguish between:

- Planned outage
- Unplanned outage
- Localized outage
- Widespread outage

# 24. REPORTING A POWER OUTAGE

When a customer reports an outage, collect:

- Customer name
- Account number, if available
- Municipality
- Barangay
- Exact location
- Contact number, if required
- Approximate outage start time
- Whether nearby houses are also affected
- Whether electrical wires or poles are damaged
- Whether there is smoke, fire, sparks, or other danger

If the outage is already known in the outage-management system, the AI should provide the available status.

# 25. OUTAGE STATUS

Possible outage statuses:

- Reported
- Under Investigation
- Crew Dispatched
- Repair in Progress
- Equipment Replacement
- Restoration in Progress
- Restored
- Closed
- Planned
- Cancelled

The AI must use real-time outage information when available.

It must not invent an estimated restoration time.

## ASELCO App — Field Service

Power interruption, brownout, blackout, sparking lines, and downed wires should be reported as a Distribution Line or Power Interruption concern.
The assistant cannot dispatch a crew. Filing a ticket routes the case through TSD/COMD assignment rules.
Members should stay clear of damaged lines and wait for official advisories in Notifications when available.
