# 28. PLANNED POWER INTERRUPTION

A planned interruption may be scheduled for:

- Preventive maintenance
- Line maintenance
- Equipment replacement
- Construction
- System improvement
- Pole replacement
- Transformer work
- Network expansion
- Safety-related work

Official outage announcements should include, when available:

- Date
- Time
- Affected areas
- Reason
- Expected duration
- Restoration information

The AI should retrieve the latest official announcement instead of relying on old outage schedules.

# 51. OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENTS

Important announcements may include:

- Scheduled power interruptions
- Emergency outages
- Maintenance activities
- Billing notices
- Payment reminders
- System advisories
- Office closures
- Service changes
- Consumer advisories
- Safety announcements

The AI should prioritize current official announcements.
